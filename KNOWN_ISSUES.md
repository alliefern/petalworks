# Known issues and limitations

Honest list. Nothing here is disguised as finished.

## Art
- **All sprites are interim.** `assets/interim/` is generated by `tools/make_interim_art.py`. It respects the palette and reads clearly, but it is not the painterly gouache look the brief asks for. The art team's files drop into `assets/art/` and replace stand-ins by name with no code change. At the time of writing `assets/art/` is empty.
- Interim art exists only for the east facing of beds and machines; other facings are runtime rotations, so the top-left light direction is wrong on rotated structures until the four-facing files arrive.
- The UI icons (coin, order slip, trowel, etc.) are also interim.

## Gameplay
- No generated orders after order 6. The sandbox is an open market (goods sell on arrival) rather than endless orders.
- The keyboard cursor announces the tile under it through a live region, but there is no per-tile tour of the whole garden for screen readers.
- Balance has been checked against `docs/design/BALANCE.md` and the Vitest affordability test, not against human playtests.

## Platform
- Desktop mouse and keyboard only. Touch is not implemented.
- No service worker; the game needs its first load online (nothing is fetched after that).
- Safari older than 18.4 falls back to MP3; the three loops (music, machine hum, belt cadence) may have a tiny seam there.
- Playwright runs against the system Chrome; it does not install its own browser.

## Copy
- Strings are placeholders in `src/ui/strings.ts`, keyed like `docs/content/copy.json`. The producer's copy pass has not been wired in yet.
