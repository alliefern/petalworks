# Petalworks — first playable checklist

For Allie, and for anyone else trying the game who does not write code.

This is a first look at the first build you can actually play: grow daisies, send them along belts, bundle them, and ship an order. It should take about fifteen minutes. You do not need the terminal, and you do not need the long producer script (`ACCEPTANCE_TEST_PLAN.md` in this folder). That script is for a full build, later.

If the producer sent a link, open that. You should see a small garden and a bar along the top with your coins and a customer order. If you see a blank page, an error box, or a sheet of grey squares, stop and send that back. There is nothing to play yet.

There is no character to walk around. You are arranging the workshop.

## What a good fifteen minutes feels like

The garden looks painted and calm. You always know what the customer is asking for, because it is written in words. Flowers grow, ride the belts one at a time, and turn corners. A machine only finishes a bundle when the right flowers have actually arrived. Shipping an order feels like a small celebration, and your coins go up once. If you refresh the page, your garden is still there.

## Try these

Tick what you did. A short note in your own words is enough when something feels off. Say what you clicked and what you expected.

- [ ] Read the first order. It should ask for daisies in plain words, show how many you have delivered, and show how many coins you will earn.
- [ ] If a tutorial appears, follow it for a minute. Then skip it, and find the button that opens it again.
- [ ] Hover a building in the build panel. It should tell you what it is for and what it costs before you spend anything.
- [ ] Place a daisy bed. A preview should show where it will land, and it should look different on a bad spot (on top of something else, or off the garden) than on a good spot.
- [ ] Turn the bed so its exit points at a belt. The R key does this. There may also be a rotate button.
- [ ] Drag a line of belts. For this first order, run the bed straight to the packing station. Leave the bundling table out of the path until the order asks for bundles. If a bundler is in the middle, daisies get tied up and the daisy order will not move, which is correct.
- [ ] Leave a one-tile gap in the belt on purpose. The flower should stop at the end of the belt. It should not hop the gap. Then fill the gap and watch it continue.
- [ ] Wait and watch. You should be able to see the bed growing, a flower ready to leave, and (if you block the exit) the bed waiting because it is stuck. Those should be easy to tell apart even if you ignore color.
- [ ] Ship the daisies the order asks for. The count should go up only when a flower reaches packing, not while it is still on the belt.
- [ ] When the order completes, notice the celebration and the coins. Then press undo (Z, or the undo button) right away. The finished order should stay finished, and the coins should stay where they are.
- [ ] Change the layout for the next order: bed, belts, bundling table, belts, packing. A bundle should appear only after two daisies have gone in. Watch that bundle ride to packing.
- [ ] Click the bundling table. You should see what it needs, how far along it is, what is sitting inside it, and which way the product comes out.
- [ ] Pause. Flowers and progress should freeze. Start again. They should continue.
- [ ] Try the speed controls, including the fastest. Flowers should move faster and still stay in their own squares. Put the speed back to normal when you are done looking.
- [ ] Turn sound off, and music off if they are separate. The garden should go quiet. Turn them back on if you want the chime when an order completes.
- [ ] Refresh the page while a flower is halfway along a belt. You should get the same garden back: same machines, same coins, same order, that flower once.
- [ ] Refresh again right after an order pays you. It should not pay you a second time.
- [ ] Try to remove a belt that has a flower sitting on it. Either the game says no and leaves everything in place, or the flower shows up somewhere obvious (a basket or a pile you can use). It should not simply disappear.
- [ ] Try to put a building where it cannot go, and, if you can get your coins low, try to buy something you cannot afford. The game should say no in words and leave your coins alone.
- [ ] Click every button you can see once. Skip anything that says New Game unless you are ready to lose the garden. Every button should do what it looks like it does.
- [ ] If lavender is already in this build, try one lavender order the same way: grow it, move it, ship it. If there is no lavender yet, write "not in this build" and move on.

## Look out for these

Any one of these is worth sending back, with a sentence about what you were doing.

- [ ] A flower vanishes, a twin appears, or two flowers sit in the same square.
- [ ] A flower jumps across empty grass.
- [ ] You get stuck: not enough coins to build what the order needs, and removing things does not give you a way forward.
- [ ] A button looks clickable and nothing happens.
- [ ] The only way you can tell a machine is stuck is by a color change, with no symbol or words.
- [ ] The order is only a tiny picture, or the writing is hard to read.
- [ ] A popup covers the garden and makes you click OK while you were just watching the belts.
- [ ] After a refresh, your coins changed on their own, a machine doubled, or the garden is empty.
- [ ] Undo right after an order puts the coins back or makes the order un-finish.
- [ ] At the fastest speed, flowers stack up or an order completes without the goods actually arriving.
- [ ] You switch to another browser tab for half a minute, come back, and the garden is buried in flowers or showing an error.
- [ ] The scene looks like a diagram: plain boxes, emoji, or ordinary unstyled form buttons. A painted stand-in is fine. A programmer sketch is not.
- [ ] The writing in the game uses British spelling. It should say "color" and "cozy".
- [ ] The game asks you to move a little person around the garden.
- [ ] Coins tick down by themselves while you watch.

## When you are done

You are done when you have shipped the daisy orders this build actually contains, refreshed once in the middle of a delivery, and written down anything that felt confusing or broken.

Send three things:

1. Did you finish the orders, or where did you stop?
2. What felt good?
3. What felt wrong, and what were you clicking when it happened?

Screenshots help. You do not need to name the cause.
